Teaching in practice

In my classroom

My classroom practice is structured around making mathematical thinking visible, checking understanding carefully and moving pupils towards confident independent work.

01

Explain clearly

Careful modelling, precise mathematical language and worked examples that make each stage of a method visible.

02

Check understanding

Targeted questioning, mini-whiteboards and misconception checking to establish what pupils understand before moving on.

03

Practise deliberately

Guided practice followed by increasingly independent work so that accuracy, fluency and mathematical confidence can develop.

04

Adapt responsively

Scaffolding, prompts and appropriate challenge informed by pupils' starting points without lowering academic expectations.

05

Build independence

High expectations alongside appropriate support, with the aim of helping pupils reason, persevere and solve problems independently.
